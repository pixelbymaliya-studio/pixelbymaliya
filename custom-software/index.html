FEATURED ENTERPRISE CASE STUDY

Pixel Distribution Suite — 14-Module Commercial DMS

Wholesale & FMCG Distribution · Sri Lanka

The Problem

Wholesale distributors in Sri Lanka operating multi-van route sales often rely on handwritten bill books and phone calls. By the time delivery vans return to the central warehouse in the evening, discrepancies between physical stock and collected cash/cheques lead to stock shrinkage, delayed invoices, and unmonitored credit limit breaches.

The Solution

Pixel by Maliya developed the Pixel Distribution Suite: a centralized single-page web platform featuring 14 unified operational modules. The system connects the central warehouse hub (e.g. Kelaniya) with branch depots, manages van dispatch manifests, tracks retail outlet credit aging, and records cash/cheque collections with automated ledger reconciliation.

Key Features Delivered

Multi-depot stock transfers & adjustments
Van sales dispatch & return manifests
Outlet master directory & credit ceilings
Executive KPI sales & revenue charts

Technologies Used

Single Page Architecture (SPA) Modern JavaScript Client-Side State Engine Local Database Sync Interactive SVG Visuals
Verified Result

Deployed and live at pixelbymaliya.lk/distribution/ with 14 interactive modules simulating full depot, van dispatch, outlet billing, and commercial operations.

Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Software in Sri Lanka

Common questions from Sri Lankan business leaders considering custom software solutions.

Why should I choose custom software instead of off-the-shelf software?

Generic software forces your company to fit into rigid, pre-made workflows that often clash with Sri Lankan tax, credit collection, and localized delivery practices. Furthermore, foreign SaaS tools require high monthly USD subscription fees that increase with currency fluctuations. Custom software is 100% tailored to your business, with zero per-user subscription penalties.

Can mobile delivery or sales apps function without continuous internet?

Yes. We engineer offline-first client engines for delivery drivers and field sales reps. Sales orders, signatures, and stock updates are cached locally on the device and automatically synchronized as soon as the vehicle enters an area with cellular data coverage.

How do you safeguard our sensitive financial and customer data?

We adhere to industry-standard data security best practices: encrypted HTTPS data transit, role-based user access controls (restricting who can see profit margins or customer debt), automated daily database backups, and strict database security rules.

Do you provide training and post-launch maintenance?

Yes. Every project includes comprehensive staff onboarding, administrator training, and ongoing technical support via direct WhatsApp and phone. We ensure your team is completely confident using the software.
