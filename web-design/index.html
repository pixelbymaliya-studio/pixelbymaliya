Frequently Asked Questions About Web Design in Sri Lanka

Everything you need to know before launching your business website with Pixel by Maliya.

How much does a website design cost in Sri Lanka?

The cost depends on the scope of the project: a clean single-page landing page, a multi-page corporate profile, or a complete e-commerce store with product catalogues and payment options. We offer transparent, tailored quotes based on your exact business needs with zero hidden charges. Contact us on WhatsApp or request a free quote for an accurate estimate.

Do you help with .LK domain registration and cloud hosting?

Yes. We guide you through selecting and connecting your official `.lk` or `.com` domain name, configuring DNS records, setting up free SSL certificates (HTTPS), and deploying to secure, fast cloud infrastructure so your site is always available.

Will my website work properly on mobile phones and tablets?

Absolutely. Over 80% of web traffic in Sri Lanka comes from mobile devices. Every website we build is developed mobile-first, ensuring smooth navigation, legible typography, and quick tap targets on all iPhone, Android, and tablet screens.

Can I accept online payments or direct WhatsApp orders?

Yes. We integrate local Sri Lankan payment gateways (such as PayHere, commercial bank IPGs, or direct bank transfer slips) as well as direct WhatsApp checkout routing where customers send their order details with one tap.

How long does it take to design and launch my website?

Most standard business websites are completed and launched within 5 to 14 business days, depending on content preparation and feedback rounds. We maintain regular communication throughout the process and provide a live staging preview for review before public launch.
