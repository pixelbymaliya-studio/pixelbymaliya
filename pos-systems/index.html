FEATURED SOFTWARE CASE STUDY

Pixel Universal POS Suite — Hybrid Offline-First Architecture

Retail & Supermarket · Sri Lanka

The Problem

Sri Lankan retail merchants frequently experience unexpected power cuts and fibre broadband disruptions. Traditional cloud-only POS systems completely freeze when the connection drops, halting billing and causing customer walkouts at checkout counters. Meanwhile, legacy desktop POS software lacked cloud backup and remote owner monitoring.

The Solution

Pixel by Maliya engineered the Pixel Universal POS engine using a hybrid offline-first client architecture. The entire product catalog, barcode index, customer ledgers, and transactions run inside the local browser storage (IndexedDB). Transactions are billed instantaneously with zero latency, and changes automatically synchronize with the cloud once the connection is restored.

Key Features Delivered

Zero-downtime offline cashier billing
Direct 58mm/80mm ESC/POS printing
Automatic background cloud sync
Real-time profit & cashier X/Z reports

IndexedDB Client Engine Modern JavaScript (ES6+) Web Thermal Printing Protocol Cloud Firestore Sync Chart.js Analytics

Frequently Asked Questions About POS Systems in Sri Lanka

Everything you need to know about setting up and running your POS with Pixel by Maliya.

Does the POS system continue working when the internet cuts out?

Yes. Pixel Universal POS is built with an offline-first architecture. It stores products, pricing, and transactions locally in your browser's IndexedDB database. You can continue scanning barcodes, billing customers, and printing receipts with zero interruption. Once your internet reconnects, transactions sync automatically to the cloud.

Can I test the POS before purchasing?

Yes! You can test our interactive live demo right now at pixelbymaliya.lk/pos/ on your phone, tablet, or PC without creating an account or installing any software.

What hardware do I need to get started?

All you need is any device with a modern web browser (a computer, laptop, or Android tablet). To print receipts and scan items, you can connect any standard 58mm or 80mm thermal receipt printer and USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner. We can also assist you with purchasing and configuring compatible hardware packages.

Can I import my existing product inventory?

Yes. We provide easy Excel and CSV inventory import templates so you can upload your full product catalog with barcodes, categories, cost prices, selling prices, and reorder levels in a single step.
